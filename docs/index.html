01 / THE DATA

A small dataset.
A demanding problem.

We use three-channel abdominal electrohysterogram measurements and follow the electrical patterns recorded during pregnancy. Class imbalance and repeated signal windows are central to how the experiments are interpreted.

300recordings
262term births
38preterm births
20Hzsampling rate

Reading the cohort

Preterm recordings account for 12.7% of the database. Overall accuracy must therefore be read alongside sensitivity, specificity and the exact unit used for training and testing.

02 / APPROACH

From signal
to decision.

The analysis begins with filtered EHG recordings and preserves both the feature-based learning and the sequence models used in the study.

01
≋

Prepare

Three bipolar channels, digital band-pass filtering and 750-sample windows with a 125-sample stride.

02
∑

Describe

Median and peak frequency, root mean square, zero crossings, variance and spectral descriptors.

03
⌁

Learn

Forward selection, ten classical classifiers, a five-model voting ensemble, CNNs, LSTMs and sparse encoding.

04
◎

Evaluate

Ten-fold prediction, complete confusion matrices and separate recording-level and window-level protocols.
